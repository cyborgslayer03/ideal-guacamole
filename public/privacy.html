Omniverse Forge: Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Omniverse Forge is a superhero universe creator and action game. This policy explains what data the game handles, on your device and on the optional online Multiverse Hub.

Data that stays on your device

Your universes, characters, stories, campaigns, settings, daily challenge scores, and any 3D model files you import are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. They are never sent anywhere unless you choose to publish something to the Multiverse Hub. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

The Multiverse Hub (optional)

You can play without an account. If you create a Hub account to share content, the Hub stores:

  • Your handle (a public username you choose) and your password, stored only as a salted scrypt hash.
  • Sign-in sessions, stored as hashed tokens, which expire after 30 days or when you sign out.
  • Content you publish (universes, characters, stories, items), with its description and tags. Published content is public: anyone can view and download it.
  • Your likes and reports on other people's content.

The Hub uses your IP address briefly, in memory, to limit request rates and prevent abuse. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs. We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or photos.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We never sell or share your data with third parties.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your Hub account at any time. Deleting it permanently erases your account, your sessions, everything you published, and your likes and reports.

  • In the app: open the Multiverse Hub, then tap Delete account and confirm with your password.
  • On the web: open the web version of the game, enter the Multiverse Hub, sign in, and choose Delete account.

Data on your device can be deleted from the home screen (delete universes) or by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the Hub doesn't knowingly collect data from them.
