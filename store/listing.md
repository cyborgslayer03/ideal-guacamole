You are the One Above All. Forge entire universes of heroes, villains, factions, worlds and stories, then step inside and fight as your creations in a fast action RPG.

CREATE ANYTHING
• Design heroes and villains with costumes, stats, gear and 28 power types: optic blasts, web shots, telekinesis, reality warping, time stops, portals, giant growth and more
• Recruit 13 ready-made Legends, or roll a whole random universe in one tap
• Write branching stories where your choices change what happens next
• Import your own 3D character models

FIGHT YOUR WAY
• Combos, dodges, ultimates and free-aimed movement: swing, leap, teleport or open portals exactly where you want
• Team up with up to three AI heroes who fight on their own, revive you, and set up team combos
• Multi-phase boss battles that enrage, summon help and rain down hazards
• Smash cars and crates, or hurl them with telekinesis

A LIVING WAR TABLE
• Rotating bosses: defeat a villain and a lieutenant takes their place
• Nemeses who remember you, allies you earn, and villain schemes on a countdown
• Loot, gear upgrades and power mastery trees

AND MORE
• A new Daily Challenge every day with score streaks
• Photo mode with comic-book filters and captions
• Share universes, characters and stories with other players in the Multiverse Hub, or keep everything offline

No ads. No tracking.
